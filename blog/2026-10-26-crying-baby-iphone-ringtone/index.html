my crying baby iPhone ringtone

Two sounds make everyone in a room look up: their own name, and a crying baby. In 2008 I picked the second one for my ringtone. I found a recording on Freesound, trimmed it to 30 seconds in iTunes, and put it on my first iPhone. Eighteen years later it still clears a meeting room.

Download babycry2.m4a (AAC, 30 seconds, under half a megabyte).

The ringtone goes out under the same CC BY 4.0 license. Anyone passing it on has to keep that attribution. The original is 41 seconds long, and n2p5 recorded it “on a DAT recorder with an AKG C3000”.

To install it, download the .m4a in Safari, open it in the Files app, tap Share, then “Use as Ringtone” (it may hide under “More”). That needs iOS 26 or later. The cap is 30 seconds, which is why the trim.

P.S. I still find it a shame that installing a ringtone is this fiddly, all to protect a business model from 20 years ago.

P.P.S. On older iOS, drop the same file renamed to babycry2.m4r onto the phone in Finder (iTunes on Windows), or import the .m4a into GarageBand on the phone and share it as a ringtone.
